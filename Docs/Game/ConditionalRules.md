## Configuration and priority

**Body turning — Thomas, 2026-10-20 follow-up:** A/D and ordinary resolved horizontal movement turn the body; idle retains that facing. Mouse/automatic Aim still owns the Revolver independently. A formal short Move preserves its starting body facing while traveling Forward or Back, so Back remains a backward step. This replaces aim-driven body facing in conditional Duel/Training only.

**Move to Platform — Thomas, 2026-10-20:** J04 retains required **Higher / Lower** under Legs/Jump; the catalog has 21 actions and the same 26 conditions / five-rule budget. Ordinary Jump remains unchanged. Rules display `Move to Platform (Higher) ↑` or `Move to Platform (Lower) ↓` throughout editing, setup and combat; copies and fight snapshots preserve the direction.

J04 claims Move and Jump for one complete transition. Before acceptance, its Jump row uses normal Jump priority; a simultaneously executable Move row ahead of it in displayed order takes precedence, while J04 overrides a lower Move row. After acceptance, ordinary rule rematching cannot preempt either claim. All other operations retain independent arbitration. Unavailable destinations release both claims to lower rows/manual input.

Selection checks active collider support geometry, actual player width, speed, jump speed, gravity, landing clearance and intervening terrain. Prefer the next reachable height, then the shortest horizontal approach/flight; ties use stable support/sample order. Higher uses a bounded supported approach and an ordinary jump. Lower may use the same current-support drop-through as manual S, walk off an edge, or jump to a lower support, including the floor. There is no transform write, jump boost, authored-coordinate shortcut or general graph route.

An accepted transition freezes its destination and retains Move/Jump until landing even after conditions or events expire. Ordinary higher-priority rows cannot interrupt it. Dodge, knockback, invalid geometry/tuning, unreachable destination or a bounded failed attempt can release it. A stuck/timed-out route is not retried at the same position and geometry until the state clears or the player moves; there is no added cooldown. After a successful landing, a still-true state may choose a new transition. Events are consumed once per row. Pause and lifecycle cleanup preserve ownership consistency. Moving/rotated platforms and arbitrary shapes are not certified by this pass.

Five operations arbitrate independently: Move and Jump (Legs), Aim and Fire (Arms), Dodge (Spine). **MaxRulesTotal = 5** is one shared budget, freely distributed. Initial rules are empty. There is no Always condition, Manual/Auto mode, enable switch, preinstalled rule or autonomous fallback.

Each rule has one required primary state or event, at most one optional additional **state**, and one action. No second event, OR/NOT, nesting, variables, arbitrary waits or separate Combo Editor. Actual actions create events that other rules can use. Manual and automatic actions share the same real event sources.

Within one operation, displayed order selects the first matching **executable** rule. A temporarily unavailable row permits lower rows; no executable row means manual control. Different operations may run together. Consumption is independent per row, even when two rows observe the same occurrence. Accepted physical Jump/Dodge, started Short Burst and platform-transfer commitments survive ordinary condition/priority changes. Different operations have no additional priority hierarchy.

Retain the body preview, category selector with one description area, and persistent right-hand rules. Allocate their widths from available space with readable minimums; the current five-rule gameplay budget is independent of view capacity. A fixed full-rule preview below Rule Setup stays outside scrolling lists. It shows selected content and concise missing-item placeholders throughout editing. Click A, B or C to edit that part; **+ Condition** adds one optional state. Only states appear in that picker. Neutral selection marks the current part; dashed temporary candidate previews from hover/keyboard focus never write to the draft. A completed rule remains visible with brief body/row commit feedback; no separate editing-status text is shown.

Ground State, Horizontal Distance, Incoming Attack, Move (Forward / Back), Keep Moving (Left / Right) and Move to Platform (Higher / Lower) expose their fixed local branch choices. **Fire 1 Shot**, **Keep Firing** and **Short Burst** have no parameters or extra confirmation. Ammo Use and its reserve behavior are removed. Descriptions, incompatibility reasons and required parameters share one detail region. Player guidance stays concise: **States last while true; events act briefly; complete actions finish their current round.** Firing versus Shot Fired and Reloading versus Reload Finished remain distinct.

Both right-hand lists show **Rules {count}**, continuous global numbering and compact expression cards. Use `A + B` above `→ C` in narrow cards, or one line when the full expression fits. Text is measured at fixed readable sizes; card height follows wrapping. The editor retains compact edit, Up/Down and Delete controls with hover/focus explanations. Add Rule and Start Fight stay in the fixed bottom row. Add reads `MaxRulesTotal`; capacity is not hard-coded to five rows. Independent masked vertical scroll views handle options and rule cards; overflow alone shows a scrollbar. Both lists follow global `DisplayOrder`. Cross-operation editing replaces the original row even at the budget limit.
All edits remain in a copied local draft. Cancel/Escape restores the original full rule, including both conditions and all parameters. Back, keyboard focus and local scroll position remain available. Incomplete editing blocks Start Fight. `Copy()`, add, replace, reorder, fight snapshots, retry and next wave preserve the full rule. Invalid over-budget data is rejected rather than truncated. Catalog owns options, descriptions, State/Event metadata, complete-rule validation and display text; UI/HUD/runtime do not maintain separate catalogs.

Keep existing six body contours and proportions. Arms are cyan, Legs mint and Spine violet. **Fire 1 Shot** requests one precise round per accepted occurrence. **Keep Firing** requests precise single rounds at the primary interval while the complete state remains true, including after automatic reload; event responses retain their 0.5-second acceptance deadline. **Short Burst** (F02) calls the same rapid, per-shot-spread process as manual RMB. Its first successful paid shot snapshots all remaining rounds, including itself. Zero rounds cannot accept or complete. The weapon exclusively owns its budget, cadence, completion and source; no Controller-side second burst simulation exists. Trigger/primary/additional-state loss and higher Hold Fire cannot interrupt an accepted burst. Aim, Move, Jump and Dodge remain independent. Each shot uses the latest resolved Aim plus fresh uniform spread, at the current actual muzzle. The last paid shot raises ShotFired, then BurstFinished exactly once, then ReloadStarted. It never crosses reload. Death, disable and encounter cleanup cancel without completion; pause freezes it. Events enter the next normal solve, never recursive rule execution. Manual consecutive single shots are not bursts.

All Fire actions remain parameterless with no confirmation or ammo suffix. Per Thomas's explicit follow-up, Keep Two Rounds is **not restored**: old AmmoUse enum/fields remain inert compatibility data, normalize to zero on copies and never reserve rounds. Keep IDs, other fields, priority and independent occurrence consumption. Hold Fire owns legal zero output and blocks new manual/automatic requests while leaving accepted bursts, reloads and emitted bullets intact. Temporarily unavailable unstarted rows retain lower/manual fallback.

`ConditionalCatalog.IncompatibilityReason(operation, rule)` validates the complete rule and relevant parameters for UI commit, Start Fight and runtime snapshots. Structural impossibilities may be grey: Airborne with a ground-only Jump, contradictory Grounded AND Airborne, mutually exclusive distances for the same enemy, and Reloading with an actual shooting action. **Reloading → Hold Fire is legal and executable. Enemy Near Aim Direction → Fine-Tune Aim is fully legal without an additional state.** Cooldowns, current targets, collision and resources remain runtime checks. Do not prohibit strong combinations or real feedback loops.

## Execution, events and ownership

`ConditionalController` resolves the five-operation input frame from public observations and gated raw `PlayerInputReader` input. Existing movement, shooting and Dodge executors accept actions and spend resources. Fire shoots using final Aim; all Enemy in Firing Line conditions instead inspect the same current physical muzzle ray captured before this frame's rule resolution. D03 sees resolved Move. Aim does not fire and Fire does not change aim. Successful Dodge suppresses a same-frame Jump and retains its original committed direction. Overridden manual press edges are not queued.

Both conditions must match. If both constrain an enemy, intersect their candidate sets so the **same enemy/source** satisfies both facts, subject to each condition's visibility requirements. Do not combine two Bosses' unrelated facts. Explicit damage/hit/threat sources remain associated with their event; a visible public projectile can identify its source without revealing a concealed body. Enemies on Both Sides remains global. Q01 Nearest and Q02 Lowest-Health retain their own explicit visible-target strategies rather than becoming source-target Aim. Player rules consider on-screen publicly visible bodies without terrain LOS rejection from platforms; Camo and genuinely unobservable bodies remain excluded. Event source binding retains its own legality and does not reveal concealed bodies. This player-only observation change does not alter Boss LOS/perception. Boss policy receives no rules, private input or hidden player intentions.

Each real event has an identity, occurrence time and necessary source. Rows consume independently only when their action is accepted. One public `ConditionalDefaults.RuleEventResponseDuration = 0.5` defines both **event lifetime from the actual event** and **ordinary event-response duration from actual action acceptance**, including Took Damage. Primary event and additional state must overlap for acceptance. An additional state becoming true during the event lifetime may enable the action, but cannot revive a consumed/expired event. Running ordinary responses still require their additional state, and new events never refresh their acceptance deadline. Hold Position/Fire count as accepted zero output. All timers use the same scaled combat clock, so pause freezes both. New events enter normal frame resolution, never recursive callback evaluation.

Finite actions accept once per uninterrupted effective state occurrence. Cooldown recovery, ordinary rematching or failed execution does not invent a new occurrence. State-triggered ordinary continuous actions last while their complete condition is true; event-triggered ordinary actions last 0.5 seconds from acceptance while their additional state remains true. Repeat Jumps retains legal grounded repetition within that response. Short Burst, platform transfer and accepted Jump/Dodge finish their actual action rather than the 0.5-second response window. Short Move uses distance and its continuing-state rules below. Real Shot Fired → Fire 1 Shot and Landed → Jump Once loops remain legal and bounded by actual cadence/resources/physics.

Firing starts only from an actual paid shot. A manual/F01 single remains active at most through that shot's primary recovery, even if LMB stays held. An accepted burst stays active between its internal rounds and ends on completion. F03 stays active between normal primary shots only while execution continues. Reload, stop and cleanup end it; dry requests and matches never start it. Manual, single-shot, burst and continuous fire can all generate public events; manual actions keep neutral visual feedback. Do not duplicate existing ShotFired notifications used by Boss observation. Initializing actors, preparation, retry or reading a snapshot must not invent action events. Dispose/fight end clears subscriptions and stale references.

Movement actions remain horizontal. Approach Enemy stops within a small horizontal alignment tolerance and resumes only after the target clearly leaves it, including targets above/below. Retreat retains eligible targets and physical collision. Keep Mid Range releases Move within its existing band; Move Toward Center releases on arrival. **Move (Forward / Back)**, M04, travels the existing 1.5-unit short distance relative to actual visible facing at acceptance, freezes that direction, and needs no enemy. A state primary and its optional state must remain true; an event primary launches once and only its optional state must remain true thereafter. Event expiry alone cannot stop it. Success emits Move Finished once; condition failure, collision, timeout or other failure never completes it. A continuously true state cannot restart the short move. **Keep Moving (Left / Right)**, M11, remains ordinary continuous movement with its own independent direction parameter.

**Hold Position** owns Move with zero ordinary horizontal output, suppressing lower/manual movement without freezing gravity or cancelling accepted Dodge/knockback. **Hold Fire** owns Fire with false output, suppressing lower/manual shooting even with no ammo or during reload. It does not recall bullets or cancel an existing reload. Ownership and feedback must represent these valid zero-output actions without fake movement or shot events.

Jump Once uses the real ground-only jump executor. Repeat Jumps may request a new jump after a legal landing while its state remains true; there is no double jump. D01 uses a valid public threat/source direction and fails without one. D02 uses a visible enemy. D03 uses resolved Move or the last movement direction when stationary. D04 uses the original manual world aim point's horizontal side, unaffected by automatic Aim.

Manual and automatic Dodge share the executor's registered-Boss pass-through rules in both preflight and physical execution; terrain still blocks them and ordinary Move cannot pass through Bosses.

**Fine-Tune Aim** chooses the visible enemy closest in angle to raw player aim, with stable retention while that intent stays clear. It never selects by player distance after the angular gate, feeds assisted output back into intent, or predicts outside the small correction cone. Q01/Q02 retain their separate all-visible nearest/lowest-health ordering. No new pairing restriction is added.

**Manual S drop-through** is a press edge through the current one-way support only, using the same capability as automatic Lower. Holding S cannot repeatedly fall through layers; lower supports catch the player and the floor stays solid. New Input System gates for configuration, pause, F8 and focus apply, with existing successful-Dodge/Jump arbitration. No global terrain-layer ignore or teleport is allowed. Restore pair collisions after clearing the support and on death/retry/wave/disposal/disable. Drop-through and edge descent create no Jump Started/Apex; real landing still creates Landed.

Normal weapon tuning is Revolver **30 damage / 6 rounds / .50 s primary / 1.80 s auto reload / .10 s burst / +/-10 degrees burst spread**; Move **7**, Jump **12**, gravity scale **3**; Dodge **distance 3 / movement .18 s / immunity .20 s / start cooldown 2.0 s**. No action writes transforms, damage, cooldowns or outcomes. Bullets still cross terrain and respect allied Boss blocking; physical movement/melee collision and current Boss combat remain intact.

Start Fight deep-copies all rule fields. Combat/pause cannot edit them. Retry/next wave retain rules and order with fresh actors, resources, observations, occurrences and feedback. New Game/Restart clears rules. Preparation remains stationary for three seconds. Simultaneous death is defeat; both Bosses must die to clear wave three. Pause/F8 freezes timers and feedback; resume does not replay old events.
