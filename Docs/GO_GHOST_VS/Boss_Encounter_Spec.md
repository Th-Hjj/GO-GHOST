Current authority: Thomas's **2026-10-05 solo Boss pressure / gunner mobility upgrade**, including his correction that Drop Cut pushes the player while the sword keeps its locked landing. It extends Optical Camo, Player Legs Platform Use + Light Duo and the focused Boss pressure pass. This specification records confirmed scope and implemented source. Drop planning samples continuous standable points as well as existing navigation nodes, simulates the approach/tell/descent and ranks expected contact positions. At the end of non-cooperative preparation it may correct once within half a platform width on the same support height, using fresh delayed visible facts and a physically reachable descent. The full 0.16 s lock then freezes the target. The motor caps horizontal descent travel at that fixed x; gravity, walls, platforms and actor contacts still apply. A real health-reducing hit requests one player push away from the landing x (facing breaks an exact tie): initial tuning is 0.35 platform width / 0.14 s, currently at most 1.4 units. PlayerMovement owns the swept horizontal reaction, preserves vertical motion, respects walls/one-way platforms and yields to an accepted Dodge. Invulnerable hits do not push; pause/reset clear or freeze state normally. Brief body contact allows up to 0.3 s for the push to clear, without teleporting either actor. A wall or immune body may still block landing. The optional landing cut has a 0.45 s opportunity, remains reserved in the shared threat budget, then either uses one cut and full recovery or expires.

Gun aim includes observation age, the remaining preparation/lock and ordinary bullet flight; Piercing has zero flight time. Locked directions remain fixed. Safe recovery may cache a next action, revalidated only after recovery ends. Sword tactics spend a legal close Rhythm opening before a closing attack, consider front projectile Guard, score Dash against predicted retreat, and allow Leap against moving targets or repeated misses. Refraction may commit a nearby route after 0.65 s of sustained public firing exposure or close pressure: 1.2–5 unit displacement, at most one jump, route estimate at most 1.4 s, safe support/firing angle and ally clearance. A 3.2 s movement cooldown and previous-origin penalty limit oscillation. Arrival revalidates the existing attack; no movement speed or hidden target input is added.

Camo filters the existing finite destination candidates for at least half a platform-width displacement and either a 20-degree view-angle change, an executable-jump-height-relative elevation change, or an opened ally-blocked firing lane. Destination and intended existing attack stay fixed through movement. Losing sight does not retarget a committed route. Route failure, invalidated terrain or duration expiry exits normally without granting an attack. Departure, takeoff, landing, entering close body contact and actual hits leave fixed world-space fragments; there is no periodic moving outline or per-frame proximity cue. Camo body/weapon never brighten as a whole on hit. Public hidden contacts are stationary copied snapshots whose lifetime is capped by the event (normally 0.08–0.12 s), with no hidden velocity. ## Navigation, perception and tactics

`ArenaNavigation2D` derives support surfaces from enabled Ground Box, Polygon, Edge and Composite colliders in the scene. It uses transformed collider paths, support rays and body-sized clearance; a collider's bounding-box top alone is not a platform. Walk/jump/drop links simulate the actor's acceleration, jump, gravity, air control and body shape. One-way character traversal and ordinary projectile collision remain separate.

Routes use duration costs. The motor executes movement and reports failed progress instead of moving the transform to a waypoint. A 0.5 s terrain scan detects collider/shape/transform changes; affected surfaces and crossing links are invalidated while unaffected cached links remain. This supports the static collider terrain in the assignment; it is not a general moving-platform or deformable-world system.

`BossPerception` supplies delayed public observations (0.12 s reaction, 0.8 s sight memory), visible body motion, expressed shots/reload and terrain visibility. It never supplies private player inputs, permissions, ammunition or AI intent. The implemented bounded habit model learns retreat direction after two comparable reactions and drops it after two absent expected reactions; it resets each attempt.

`BossTactics` scores finite legal candidate destinations by reachability, range/line of fire, exposure, travel, repetition and partner role. A plan carries its destination, route, target hypothesis, ability, risk, expiry and terrain version. Default decisions run at 0.2 s, hold a useful plan for at least 0.7 s and expire it after 3.2 s. The gunner repositions for firing/cover or Camo; the swordsman chooses real approach, jump/drop, rhythm, dash or guard opportunities. Low-confidence search follows reachable visible positions instead of the hidden player's live transform.

The corner correction adds finite spacing resets after close contact or unsuccessful melee sequences and collision-validated real jumps for aerial pressure or ally lane release. A new in-place plan retires the old movement command. Failed destinations are briefly avoided; lost-contact search uses the last public observation and terrain visibility. Gun aim checks three observed body points and executable gravity/terrain prediction. Rhythm uses the three explicit arcs above, with matching stickman poses and independent locks. This is attack selection and geometry, not extra damage or hidden input access.

Lost-contact search records inspected supports until a fresh visible observation or completion of a finite sweep, with a route deadline. This prevents repeatedly choosing nearby floor points whose view reaches only the stale floor memory. Player Legs retains the last copied visible bounds for a short, supported walk to a nearby viewing angle after contact expires; Arms still requires a fresh public target. Both searches preserve terrain visibility and do not read hidden actor positions.

`LegacyPlayerAI` remains the same policy across waves. It now consumes copied `BossContact` observations and all active `BossThreat` entries. Arms prefers a visible, shootable body point and retains a useful focus for 0.6 s; a blocked or invalid target can be replaced immediately. Guard affects shot feasibility. Camo observations expire, and cues expose their own position without hidden velocity; there is no live concealed Rigidbody aim input. Spine retains its 0.20 s prediction and evaluates simultaneous expressed threats and ordinary projectiles.

Legs prefers a supported inward walk near a threatened wall, then an executable jump and landing over an observed body if needed. Jump feasibility uses the full predicted trajectory, body clearance and terrain rather than rejecting it with a ground-only body check. A short commitment prevents distance preference from immediately reversing the escape. Normal threat avoidance and Spine's accepted-action checks remain active.

Legs also evaluates voluntary relocation while grounded, owning movement, outside Dodge/committed routes and immediate threat responses. After at least 1 s of public melee/body pressure and support residence, it scores nearby different supports for closest-Boss spacing, separation from Severance, other Boss body clearance, wall clearance, travel and recent-support cost. It does not require a blocked firing line and gives no generic bonus for height. Width/spacing and the executable standard jump bound the search; the existing full trajectory, landing, headroom, body and threat checks must pass. The chosen support and x stay fixed until landing; horizontal steering follows that endpoint. A safe 2.5 s platform hold, 6 s relocation cooldown and previous-support penalty prevent repeated voluntary jumps. Expressed danger and corner escape may leave sooner; Spine retains Dodge authority. Memory resets with each fresh player policy.

## Third-wave coordination

`DuoCoordinator` reserves major-threat ownership, checks ally occupancy/fire lines, scores pressure and recovery cover, and searches reachable pairs of positions. Ordinary cooperation permits one major attack while the partner approaches, guards or uses restricted Burst. Only Cross Execution (Piercing + Draw Dash) and Sweep & Drop (Sweep + real jump/Drop Cut) permit a pair of major attacks. Solo profiles, damage and full tells are unchanged in wave three.

If the gunner's available body-point rays remain blocked by its partner, it seeks another firing position and can request a real jump from the idle swordsman to open a temporary lane. The swordsman finishes committed attacks first; neither bullets nor beams gain ally penetration. This ordinary lane cooperation does not bypass the shared major-threat budget or count as a completed Cross Execution.

Both combinations progress through Assemble, Ready, committed actions and Recover. Assembly uses actual route/recovery/braking time plus a small decision allowance, with a 1.2 s minimum and a cap derived from one standard jump, one platform-width walk and braking (never above 2.25 s). Assembly allows at most one non-walk transition per actor; Sweep & Drop's attack jump is accounted for by its existing attack timing instead. Evaluation starts 2 s into combat. After both full recoveries, a successful pair has 0.75 s common respite and a 6 s shared cooldown. Failure releases pending work, permits normal combat and retries after 1 s. Locked attacks remain committed; a death immediately releases future cooperation. A legal alternative to the last successful type is preferred, without forcing alternation.

Ordinary major-threat reservations release their blocking role during safe recovery, allowing a partner's full preparation to overlap that recovery. A dash still eligible for a follow-up is not safe recovery. Preparation/lock/active remain exclusive outside the two explicit pairings; actor collision, aim locks and readable tells remain. Combo search retains current/nearby candidates within one platform width and executable jump height, limits duplicate candidates per support and prefers less actual travel before score. It tries bounded alternate pairs when timing/escape validation rejects a candidate and may plan during safe recovery without cancelling it. Existing Drop Cut approach commitments cannot be replaced by assembly. The two combinations retain their full recovery and common respite; failure promptly returns ordinary combat.

**Editor / development-only P:** during Wave 3 combat, each press requests Cross Execution, then Sweep & Drop, alternating per attempt. It bypasses normal type preference and shared combo cooldown only. Public contact, current commitments, individual skill cooldowns, ammo, reachable positions, collision, complete tells and escape validation remain mandatory. The Console reports acceptance or a legal rejection reason. No actor placement, forced hit, UI or fake completion is added. Sweep escape sampling follows the existing wall-penetrating bullet rule.

Cross Execution freezes its retreat hypothesis before either response. With A = full dash tell + estimated first body contact, B = full beam tell and delta = 0.40 s, sword delay is max(0, B-A-delta), gun delay max(0, A+delta-B). Return Cut is disabled for this dash. Sweep & Drop considers up to three real supports, validates a normal jump, and schedules the downward blade about 0.45 s after the first sweep bullet's estimated arrival (target window 0.35–0.60 s). Travel, braking, full tells, bullet speed, blade length and descent enter these estimates; collisions can cause misses or aborts.

Finite left/right walk/jump samples reject obvious sealed routes using the new attack geometry and schedule. They are a local safeguard, not proof of universal escapability. Runtime appearance and timing remain evidence-dependent; unobserved combinations are recorded in Implementation_Log.


## Presentation and audio

Boss prefabs reuse the formal player's round-head and square-segment sprites/material. Blue/red stickmen have green/gold simple weapons, with no hair, coat or anatomical contours. `BossSilhouetteView` retains its serialized API but animates the simple limbs for locomotion, two-handed aim, recoil, crouch, jump/landing, each skill and defeat. Public threat geometry drives tells. Only small beam/blade/muzzle/spark areas use HDR; Bloom starts at threshold 1.1, intensity 0.14, scatter 0.38. The formal camera enables HDR and post-processing.

Audio uses Kenney's [Sci-fi Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/sci-fi-sounds), [RPG Audio](https://kenney.nl/assets/rpg-audio) and [Impact Sounds](https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds), all **CC0-1.0**. Original creator pages and each downloaded package's `License.txt` were checked on 2026-10-05. Thirteen selected Ogg files were imported and one existing impact reused; packages were not imported wholesale. [boss-manifest.json](../../Assets/_Game/Shared/Audio/Licenses/boss-manifest.json) records actual files, download URLs, original names, author, license, hashes and modifications; package licenses are alongside it.

`SfxSet` preserves the original ten cue indices and appends Boss events. Its 12-voice pool deduplicates by actor/attack/event, prioritizes important cues and stops interrupted/ended actor sounds. Shot, slash, lock, guard contact, Camo, movement, reload and defeat events come from actual execution. Clip import/event counters do not establish audible quality: listening/mix acceptance remains **NOT TESTED** until explicitly recorded in the implementation log.
