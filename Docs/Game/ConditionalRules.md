# Conditional rules and Body Configuration

The current rule editor pairs **one condition with one action**. The player can equip 5 rules in any order. A rule temporarily takes over its control when its condition matches and its action can execute. Other controls stay available to the player. The [formal catalog](#formal-catalog) lists the selectable entries. [Art Direction](ArtDirection.md) covers the visual feedback and the [implementation log](ImplementationLog.md) records verification.

**Jump priority — Thomas, 2026-10-26:** legal manual and automatic Jump takes priority over Dodge. A new Jump immediately interrupts an active Dodge; same-frame Jump/Dodge requests execute only Jump, regardless of the Dodge rule's DisplayOrder. Interruption ends remaining Dodge movement and immunity, preserves the original cooldown deadline and does not emit Dodge Finished. Ignored Boss body pairs restore after safe separation if the interruption occurs inside a Boss. Invalid airborne requests do not cancel Dodge or grant another jump. Ordinary Jump rules retain DisplayOrder among themselves; Move/Aim/Fire remain parallel and accepted platform routes retain their existing commitment. Pause, focus, F8 and lifecycle input gates still apply.

**Fire** offers **Keep Firing** and **Short Burst**. Short Burst (F02) commits the remaining magazine with reduced accuracy and damage, just like manual RMB.

Selection checks active collider support geometry, actual player width, speed, jump speed, gravity, landing clearance and intervening terrain. Prefer the next reachable height, then the shortest horizontal approach/flight; ties use stable support/sample order. Higher uses a bounded supported approach and an ordinary jump. Lower may use the same current-support drop-through as manual S, walk off an edge, or jump to a lower support, including the floor. There is no transform write, jump boost, authored-coordinate shortcut or general graph route.

An accepted transition freezes its destination and retains Move/Jump until landing even after conditions or events expire. Ordinary higher-priority rows cannot interrupt it. Manual Dodge, knockback, invalid geometry/tuning, unreachable destination or a bounded failed attempt can release it. A stuck/timed-out route is not retried at the same position and geometry until the state clears or the player moves; there is no added cooldown. After a successful landing, a still-true state may choose a new transition. Events are consumed once per row. Pause and lifecycle cleanup preserve ownership consistency. Moving/rotated platforms and arbitrary shapes are not certified by this pass.

Five operations share rule arbitration: Move and Jump (Legs), Aim and Fire (Arms), Dodge (Spine). **PlayerRuleLimit = 5** is one shared budget, freely distributed; `MaxRulesTotal = 20` remains the storage and validation capacity. There is no Always condition, Manual/Auto mode, enable switch or autonomous fallback.

Existing serialized rules can contain multiple conditions. All must match (AND). A new rule has one condition. Manual and automatic actions use the same event sources.

**Keep Firing** requests precise single rounds at the primary interval while the complete state remains true, including after automatic reload; event responses retain their 0.5-second acceptance deadline. **Short Burst** (F02) calls the same rapid, per-shot-spread process as manual RMB. Its first successful paid shot snapshots all remaining rounds, including itself. Zero rounds cannot accept or complete. The weapon exclusively owns its budget, cadence, completion and source; no Controller-side second burst simulation exists. Trigger/primary/additional-state loss cannot interrupt an accepted burst. Aim, Move, Jump and Dodge remain independent. Each shot uses the latest resolved Aim plus fresh uniform spread, at the current actual muzzle. The last paid shot raises ShotFired, then BurstFinished exactly once, then ReloadStarted. It never crosses reload. Death, disable and encounter cleanup cancel without completion; pause freezes it. Events enter the next normal solve, never recursive rule execution. Manual consecutive single shots are not bursts.

The Fire mode selects the underlying firing action. Old AmmoUse enum/fields remain inert compatibility data and never reserve rounds. Copies preserve unrelated fields. Removed Hold Fire (F06) is invalid and is never replaced with another action. Temporarily unavailable unstarted rows retain lower/manual fallback.

`ConditionalCatalog.IncompatibilityReason(operation, rule)` checks the selected pair, its parameters and any retained AND conditions. Airborne conflicts with Jump and Change Platform. Reloading conflicts with both Fire modes. Contradictory ground states or distance bands invalidate an older multi-condition rule. Cooldowns, targets, collision and resources are checked at execution.

## Execution, events and ownership

`ConditionalController` resolves the five-operation input frame from public observations and gated raw `PlayerInputReader` input. Existing movement, shooting and Dodge executors accept actions and spend resources. Fire shoots using final Aim. Historical A40 observes the current physical muzzle ray but is no longer a valid selectable condition. Aim does not fire and Fire does not change aim. Legal Jump precedes Dodge for automatic, manual and mixed requests. The real jump executor interrupts an active Dodge before applying jump velocity. Dodge retains its original direction until completion or interruption. Overridden manual press edges are not queued.

All conditions must match. Intersect every enemy-constraining candidate set so the **same enemy/source** satisfies all those facts, subject to each condition's visibility requirements. Do not combine two Bosses' unrelated facts. Explicit damage/hit/threat sources remain associated with their event; a visible public projectile can identify its source without revealing a concealed body. Historical A39 remains a global observation but is no longer selectable. Auto Aim selects the nearest visible enemy independently of the condition’s event source. Player rules consider on-screen publicly visible bodies without terrain LOS rejection from platforms; Camo and genuinely unobservable bodies remain excluded. Event source binding retains its own legality and does not reveal concealed bodies. This player-only observation change does not alter Boss LOS/perception. Boss policy receives no rules, private input or hidden player intentions.

Each real event has an identity, occurrence time and necessary source. Rows consume independently only when their action is accepted. One public `ConditionalDefaults.RuleEventResponseDuration = 0.5` defines both **event lifetime from the actual event** and **ordinary event-response duration from actual action acceptance**, including Took Damage. Move uses that event lifetime directly, without an extra acceptance window. Every event needs a live occurrence overlapping all other conditions at acceptance. Multiple event conditions consume their matched occurrences together, independently for each rule; repeated copies of the same event condition may use the same occurrence. A state becoming true during those windows may enable the action, but cannot revive a consumed/expired event. Running ordinary responses still require all their state conditions, and new events never refresh their acceptance deadline. All timers use the same scaled combat clock, so pause freezes both. New events enter normal frame resolution, never recursive callback evaluation.

Finite actions accept once per uninterrupted effective state occurrence. Cooldown recovery, ordinary rematching or failed execution does not invent a new occurrence. State-triggered ordinary continuous actions last while their complete condition is true; event-triggered ordinary actions other than Move last 0.5 seconds from acceptance while all their state conditions remain true. Move ends when its event conditions expire. Short Burst, platform transfer and accepted Jump/Dodge follow their actual action rather than the 0.5-second response window; legal Jump can interrupt Dodge without refunding its consumed occurrence. Move uses live condition eligibility as described below. For example, Landed → Jump can trigger again only after the next real landing.

The internal weapon firing process (no longer a selectable condition) starts only from an actual paid shot. An F01 single remains active at most through that shot's primary recovery. An accepted burst stays active between its internal rounds and ends on completion. Held manual LMB and F03 stay active between normal primary shots only while execution continues. Reload, stop and cleanup end it; dry requests and matches never start it. Manual, single-shot, burst and continuous fire can all generate public events; manual actions keep neutral visual feedback. Do not duplicate existing ShotFired notifications used by Boss observation. Initializing actors, preparation, retry or reading a snapshot must not invent action events. Dispose/fight end clears subscriptions and stale references.

**Move** (M11) uses **Toward Enemy** or **Away from Enemy**, based on a visible target. Toward Enemy holds zero input within the horizontal alignment tolerance and resumes when the target moves outside it. Move releases to a lower rule or manual input as soon as the condition or event window expires. Terrain still blocks movement.

**Keep Distance**, M03, retains the original bands and .35-unit hysteresis: Close 2.1–5.3, Mid 5.3–8.5 and Far 8.5–11.7 units. Too close retreats; too far approaches; reaching the band releases Move. Serialized `KeepRangeOption.Short/Mid/Long` values remain 1/0/2 and display Close/Mid/Far; omitted data remains Mid. Invalid values fail normal validation.

Hold Position / Stop Moving and Hold Fire are removed without substitute actions. Their old rules remain invalid until edited.

**Jump** repeats on legal landings for a sustained condition. A discrete event such as Took Damage triggers one jump per eligible occurrence. **Dodge** can move **Away from Threat**, using a public threat or source direction, or **Toward Enemy**, using a visible enemy. Both use the existing Dodge movement and cooldown.

Manual and automatic Dodge share the executor's registered-Boss pass-through rules in both preflight and physical execution; terrain still blocks them and ordinary Move cannot pass through Bosses.

**Auto Aim** (Q01) points at the nearest visible enemy’s current body center. It can turn independently of movement. Aiming and firing have separate rule owners, so the player can aim manually while a Fire rule pulls the trigger or fire manually while a rule aims.

**Manual S drop-through** is a press edge through the current one-way support only, using the same capability as automatic Lower. Holding S cannot repeatedly fall through layers; lower supports catch the player and the floor stays solid. New Input System gates for configuration, pause, F8 and focus apply, with existing successful-Dodge/Jump arbitration. No global terrain-layer ignore or teleport is allowed. Restore pair collisions after clearing the support and on death/retry/wave/disposal/disable. Drop-through and edge descent create no Jump Started/Apex; real landing still creates Landed.

Normal weapon tuning is Revolver **20 damage / 6 rounds / .45 s primary / 1.80 s auto reload / .10 s burst / +/-14 degrees burst spread**; Move **7**, Jump **12**, gravity scale **3**; Dodge **distance 3 / movement .18 s / immunity .20 s / start cooldown 1.7 s**. No action writes transforms, damage, cooldowns or outcomes. Bullets cross interior terrain, stop at the Arena perimeter and respect allied Boss blocking; physical movement/melee collision and current Boss combat remain intact.

Each combat attempt starts with a copy of the equipped rules, fresh actors and a three-second preparation countdown. **Retry** starts the same encounter directly. **Configuration** returns to preparation. Test Room is optional. The current wave route and Wave 2 metrics cutoff are documented in [Boss Encounters](BossEncounters.md).

## Configuration compatibility

The indexed condition sequence preserves the original `Condition` / `AdditionalCondition` fields and typed parameters as its first two serialized slots, with `ExtraConditions` storing the unbounded tail. Missing/null tails mean older one-/two-condition rules; they retain their original IDs and options, subject to the migrations and validation below. Copying, fight snapshots and analytics JSON retain the full sequence. Preset templates are retired.


Copies convert old action IDs into their parameterized forms before validation. M01/M02 map to Move Toward/Away from Enemy, and D02 maps to Dodge Toward Enemy. Other historical values can be preserved in data while requiring manual reselection. These include fixed-direction/Arena Center Move, Lowest Health or Aim Assist, Movement/Aim Direction Dodge, Fire 1 Shot and retired conditions. Old Jump Once and Repeat rules both use Jump. M04 Forward/Back requires a new direction. Incoming Bullet value 2 remains Ranged and the former Low value 4 is invalid. A removed condition invalidates the entire row until repaired. Row order and other fields survive migration.
